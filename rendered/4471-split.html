The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so day and month swap. “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: booking-v3 asks for the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks like the parser bug, but the parser fix does not touch the hint. It comes only through the booking form of tenants on booking-v3.

one-day move: the date moves by one day, not by months. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” A day/month swap cannot do that; a time zone or daylight-saving bug can. The desk noted these reports in desk-notes.json. The table keeps them under parser too, where the desk filed them by tenant, so the parser count matches the desk’s view.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Decision 0001 merges reports with the same subject. All three problems arrive with the same subjects, such as “Wrong day on the invite” and “Month and day swapped?”, so all of them landed in #4471.
  • Intake tags come from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. The “intake tagged as” column shows how often they disagree with the problem.
Do not close #4471, or the reports in it, when the parser fix ships. The hint and one-day move reports stay broken. Close each problem on its own ticket.

How the split is made

src/lib/kb/problems.ts counts the desk’s way: each report once (uniqueById), under its cause from causes.ts by tenant and booking form, plus a row for the reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The table is rebuilt from data on every render.
